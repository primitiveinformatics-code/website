Key Insights
Layer 1 is necessary but not sufficient. CPU, memory, and HTTP latency tell you the pipes are working. They say nothing about whether the agent is reasoning correctly.
Layer 2 reveals process failures. Span tracing across tool calls exposes wrong tool selection, retry storms, and chain corruption that Layer 1 is blind to.
Layer 3 is the hardest to instrument. Semantic quality requires LLM-as-judge, RAGAS metrics, or human evaluation. Most teams skip it — and only discover failures from user complaints.
All three layers must fire alerts independently. A Layer 3 semantic failure can occur with perfect Layer 1 and Layer 2 scores — as seen in silent hallucination events.
